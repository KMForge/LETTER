My love,

We’ve known each other since 2019, and sometimes I think about how long it took for us to get here. I tried to reach your heart more than once, but things never quite worked out. Looking back, maybe the timing wasn’t right. Maybe we both still had growing to do before we could build something we were ready to care for.

Still, through all those years, I never quite forgot you.

When I reached out again in 2026, I knew I wanted to pursue you sincerely. I wanted to get to know who you had become, to show you who I had become, and to see whether we could finally have a chance together.

I know it wasn’t easy for you to believe me at first. You were afraid that I might leave someday, and I understood why words alone weren’t enough. You needed time and reasons to trust me. I wanted my actions to give you those reasons, little by little, without asking you to ignore your fears.

Those months of courting you meant so much to me. Even when things felt uncertain, my feelings were sincere. And what makes being here so special isn’t simply that I kept trying—it’s that, in your own time, you chose me too.

Then September 19, 2026 came, and we became official.

After all those years, I could finally call you my girlfriend. Even now, that thought makes me smile. I’m grateful that we found our way back to each other, and even more grateful that this time, we both wanted to stay.

Sometimes our story reminds me of the red string theory: two people connected across time, even when their paths don’t quite meet. I don’t know whether everything happened because of fate. But I know that whatever brought us here, what we build from this point forward will come from the choices we make together.

And I want to keep choosing you.

I want a future with you—the ordinary days as much as the milestones. I want to keep learning about you, listening to you, and growing beside you. I don’t want my effort to end now that we’re together. You deserve to feel loved throughout our relationship, not only during the time I was trying to win your heart.

I know there will be challenges. We’ll have misunderstandings, difficult days, and more growing to do. I can’t promise that I’ll always get everything right, but I want to meet those moments with honesty, patience, and the willingness to take responsibility and do better. I hope we’ll remember that we’re on the same side—that we can face the problem together instead of turning against each other.

Thank you for opening your heart to me. Thank you for giving us a chance, and for loving me too.

Since 2019, so much has changed. Now, we finally get to discover what being together can become. I don’t take that lightly.

I love you, and when I imagine the future I want to work toward, I hope it’s still us—older, wiser, and still choosing each other.

With all my love.
